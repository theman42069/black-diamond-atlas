        Sources & safety

        LIDAR: USGS 3DEP (2017–2020) and PAMAP (2006–2008). Aerial photos: NHAP, USGS, NAIP, PAMAP, PEMA and PennPilot, all served by PASDA, Penn State. Slope and ground heights: USGS 3D Elevation Program. Topo maps: USGS Historical Topographic Map Collection via Esri. Satellite: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics. Place search: Esri World Geocoder. Colliery pins and railroads: Anthracite Collieries of Pennsylvania and OpenStreetMap contributors.

        Stay safe. Old mine ground can collapse, and most of it is private property. Research from home, and ask before going on anyone's land.
